# 🌍 Smart Tourism & Travel Planning Platform

An AI-powered travel planning automation platform built using **n8n** to simplify and automate the entire travel planning process. The platform collects traveler preferences, recommends destinations, generates personalized itineraries, manages bookings, sends travel reminders, and gathers customer feedback.

---

#  Problem Statement

Travel agencies often spend significant time understanding customer preferences, comparing travel options, coordinating bookings, preparing itineraries, and communicating travel updates manually. This process is time-consuming and makes it difficult to serve multiple customers efficiently.

This project automates these repetitive tasks using modular n8n workflows and AI-powered decision making.

#  Workflow Architecture

The project is divided into six independent workflows:

## Workflow 1
Customer Registration & Preference Collection

Collects traveler information including:
- Name
- Email
- Budget
- Interests
- Destination Preference

---

## Workflow 2
Destination Recommendation & Budget Planning

Uses AI to recommend destinations based on:
- Budget
- Interests
- Travel Duration
- Preferred Activities

---

## Workflow 3
Booking Management & Itinerary Generation

Creates:
- Personalized itinerary
- Hotel planning
- Transportation planning
- Daily schedule

---

## Workflow 4
Travel Alerts & Notifications

Automatically sends:
- Weather updates
- Travel reminders
- Booking confirmations
- Important notifications

---

## Workflow 5
Feedback Collection & Travel Analytics

Collects customer feedback after travel and stores analytics for future improvements.

---

## Workflow 6
Travel Feedback Analytics Workflow

Analyzes customer feedback and generates useful insights for travel agencies.
